## T1 · Taxonomy dimensions

*Taxonomy dimensions used to classify Graph Mamba architectures (manuscript tab:taxonomy-dimensions).*

| Dim. | Design question | Architectural aspect | Main question | Representative design choices | Categories |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| D1 | Tokenization | Graph representation and token construction | What information is presented to the state-space model? | Nodes, neighborhoods, walks, metapaths, spectral components, snapshots, higher-order cells | Free text (per model) |
| D2 | Ordering | Serialization, scanning, and directionality | How are graph-derived representations ordered and traversed? | Structural ranking, traversal, temporal ordering, type-aware or rank-aware ordering, uni-/bi-/multi-directional scanning | Free text (per model) |
| D3 | Coupling | Structural conditioning of state-space computation | Does graph information directly affect the selective state dynamics? | Graph-conditioned B_t, C_t, or Δ_t; modified transition; graph-aware state-space operator | Input-level, Dynamics-level, Operator-level |
| D4 | Integration | Integration with graph propagation or attention | How is the SSM combined with other graph-learning mechanisms? | Parallel local–global branches, graph encoder followed by Mamba, embedded state evolution, attention–SSM hybrids (classified by how the SSM branch is integrated) | Standalone, Sequential, Parallel, Embedded |

> The four dimensions are not mutually exclusive; a model receives one label per dimension, and multiple D3/D4 labels when it combines strategies.
